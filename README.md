App idea

I want to build an app that helps students give away or sell college items they no longer need. For example, after completing a class, a student could post a used textbook, or offer items like a mini fridge or fan — whether they’re slightly damaged or still working well. The app will make it easy for other students to find and pick up these items on campus.
